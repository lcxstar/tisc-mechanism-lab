**Material source-code defect 1 — sediment accounting:** author `float total_sed_mass` has catastrophic global accumulation loss when frequent lake deposition increments fall below ULP. The accepted 03t sidecar `double` ledger preserves physical equations but fixes published erosion, net deposit, exported sediment, and balance reporting. This proves conservation of logged source/sink/flux transactions, *not* physically stored Block sediment thickness or grid convergence.

**Material source-code defect 2 — negative slope:** author's erosion mode 6 sometimes raises a negative near-zero outlet slope to noninteger exponent 1.05 and outputs NaN. Independent 03k domain guard is a physical numerical correction and remains separately labeled from 03t precision accounting.

But real Junggar basin tectonic evolution is *not* yet reconstructed: this is synthetic prescribed kinematics.

## Three unresolved questions and discriminating experiments

1. **First divergence onset and growth.** At identical dt=0.5 Myr, dt_eros=.05 Myr and identical author tectonic inputs, run two executable variants from t=0 to each requested horizon (0.5,1,1.5,2,3,4,6,10,12,15 Myr) for N121 and selected N401. Compare integer/float fields on the *same grid*, identify first changed topology edge and first reported nonzero elevation/stratigraphy difference. Note that 0.1m rounded exported maps cannot resolve earlier microscopic internal trajectory differences. Report earliest visible difference as an upper bound, not the exact first floating-point difference.

2. **Drainage/lake threshold effects.** `demo.xyw` lists drainage type ('R','L','E'), one downstream destination pair (x-to,y-to), water and sediment, plus printed topo. Compare category counts, directed edge changes, changed catchment/outlet route fractions. `demo.lak` lists every lake's node membership, outlet nodes, elevation and discharge — compare lake counts, member sets, outlet sets and matched lake sizes, preserving matching by spatial-node overlap (numeric lake IDs need not remain stable). A sudden jump of changed edges/lake membership concurrent with a jump in RMS elevation is consistent with a hydrologic threshold, but not proof of causality; record actual first altered node, lake and period.

3. **Time and space convergence.** Paired precision variants with *each fixed input combination*: (a) `dt=0.5`, `dt_eros=.05` as author; (b) `dt_eros=.025` holding tectonic dt; (c) `dt=.25` with `dt_eros=.025`, preserving 10 erosion substeps per tectonic main step. Independently test N81,N121,N201,N401 where manageable (Nx/Ny only for grid tests). Every comparison measures difference *between float and double variant at that specific configuration* and also compares the corresponding baseline among resolutions. No comparison across grids based only on mismatched cell positions: interpolate/summarize volume and topology appropriately. Only call it convergent if differences decrease consistently as `dt` and/or cell spacing shrink, preferably at same final elapsed geologic time and with control of the hydrological discontinuities.
